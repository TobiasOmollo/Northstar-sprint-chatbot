**INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT: NORTHSTAR RETAIL CO.**
**SUBJECT: STANDARD RETURN & REFUND ELIGIBILITY RULES**
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** 15th Aug 2026
**POLICY OWNER:** Customer Support Operations
---

### 1. Purpose
To provide the Customer Support team with definitive, non-discretionary criteria for approving or denying return/refund requests. This policy ensures consistency, reduces friction for legitimate claims, and protects Northstar Retail from fraud and unsellable inventory.

### 2. The "Northstar 30-Day Window" (General Rule)
**All items are eligible for return/refund within 30 calendar days from the delivery date**, provided they meet the conditions outlined in Section 3.

- *Exception:* Items shipped via "Pre-Order" or "Backorder" have a 30-day window starting from the *ship date*, not the delivery date.

---

### 3. Condition Requirements (The "Sellable" Standard)
To be eligible for a full refund to the original payment method, the returned item **must** meet **all** of the following criteria:

| Criteria | Requirement | Support Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Packaging** | Must be returned in the original manufacturer’s box. Shipping labels must not be affixed directly to the product box (must use a poly-mailer/outer box). | Deny if box is destroyed or used as the shipping vessel. |
| **Product State** | Unused, unwashed, and free of odors (smoke, pets, perfume, mildew). | Deny if stains, wear marks, or odors are detected. |
| **Accessories** | All tags, manuals, cables, chargers, and free promotional gifts must be included. | Deduct gift value from refund if missing. |
| **Safety Seals** | Hygiene seals (e.g., for underwear, earrings, mattress toppers) must be intact. | **Immediate Deny** if broken. |

---

### 4. Non-Returnable Items (The "Final Sale" List)
**The following SKUs are strictly ineligible for return or refund under any circumstance**, except for shipping damage (see Section 6):

- **Perishables:** Food, beverages, and floral arrangements.
- **Hygiene Sensitive:** Opened beauty/cosmetics, personal care devices (trimmers, toothbrushes), and undergarments/swimwear (if hygiene liner is removed).
- **Digital Goods:** Software licenses, gift cards, and downloadable e-books.
- **Custom Orders:** Personalized jewelry, monogrammed apparel, or custom-built electronics.

---

### 5. Partial Refund Rules (Depreciation)
If an item is returned within the 30-day window but **fails** the "Sellable" standard (Section 3), Support may offer a **partial refund** at the following rates, provided the item is not a "Final Sale" item:

| Condition Found | Refund Percentage | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Damaged Packaging** (item itself is fine) | 85% of purchase price | Item can be sold as "Open Box." |
| **Missing Accessories** | 75% of purchase price | Deduct cost of replacement charger/cable. |
| **Visible Wear** (tried on, light scuff) | 50% of purchase price | Must be sold as "Used - Good." |
| **Odor/Stains/Heavy Use** | **0% (Deny & Return to Sender)** | Customer pays return shipping. |

---

### 6. Defective vs. Damaged (Shipping Claims)
**Support must differentiate between these two scenarios:**

- **Damaged in Transit (Carrier Fault):** If the outer box is crushed or torn. **Action:** Direct customer to file a claim with the carrier; Northstar does not process refunds for carrier damage unless the customer purchased "Shipping Protection."
  
- **Defective (Manufacturer Fault):** Item arrives with a dead pixel, motor failure, or broken zipper.
  - **Action:** **Always approve** a full refund or replacement within **60 days** (extended window). Do not require the customer to return the defective item if the weight exceeds 5 lbs (to save shipping costs); issue a "Returnless Refund" for heavy/bulky defects.

---

### 7. The "Change of Mind" / Remorse Rule
If the customer simply does not like the item (color, fit, size), they are eligible for a return **only if** they cover the return shipping label cost.
- **Deduction:** Northstar will deduct a flat **$7.99 restocking fee** from the refund for all remorse returns to cover handling.

---

### 8. Refund Processing Timeline
To set proper expectations for the customer:

| Milestone | Timeline |
| :--- | :--- |
| Return package scan by USPS/UPS | Refund processed within **48 hours** of scan. |
| Refund to Credit Card | 3–5 business days (bank dependent). |
| Refund to Store Credit (Gift Card) | **Instant** upon scan. |

---

### 9. The "Fraud Flag" Protocol (Support Authority)
Support agents have the authority to **instantly deny** a return/refund request if the system flags **any** of the following red flags, **without** supervisor approval:

1. **Order history** shows more than 3 returns in the last 30 days.
2. The return tracking number shows the item was shipped from a different state than the delivery address.
3. The customer requests a refund *before* providing a tracking number.
4. The return request is filed on Day 31–35. (Note: Days 36+ require Director-level approval).

---

### 10. Agent Decision Matrix (Quick Reference Card)

| Scenario | Window | Condition | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Customer hates the color | Day 10 | Unworn, tags on | Approve refund - $7.99 fee. |
| Customer washed it and it shrank | Day 5 | Washed | **Deny.** (Wash care not our liability). |
| Item arrived with scratch | Day 20 | Defective | Approve full refund + prepaid label. |
| Item is final sale jewelry | Day 2 | Unworn | **Deny.** Cite policy. |
| Customer claims empty box | Day 1 | N/A | **Do not refund.** Escalate to Fraud Team for investigation. |
